Rockfall

A puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Turn the chutes so every boulder from the quarry rolls safely into the water, and keep the stone out of the town.

Help

  • Turning chutes: tap a chute to turn it. Pink chutes are linked and turn together.
  • Moving the island: pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to turn it, and drag with one finger to look around. Double-tap the sky to see the whole island again.
  • Release all: once every route reaches the water, release the waiting boulders early for bonus points.
  • Hint: pauses play and marks the chutes to turn, for a few points.
  • Challenges: bonus points earned in a world open that world's challenge.

Buying the full game

World 1 and the Daily Quarry are free. One payment unlocks every other world, for good. If you have bought it before, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases.

Progress on more than one device

Sign in to iCloud and Game Center on each device and your progress follows you.
