Rockfall privacy

Rockfall is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Cloud Kinetic Solutions Ltd. This page explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. Rockfall has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run a server for the game, so your play never reaches us.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, bonus points, tokens), your Daily Quarry and Weekly Storm results, and your settings are kept on your device.

What Apple handles

  • If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is copied to your own iCloud account so it is the same on your other devices. We cannot see it.
  • If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores go to Game Center leaderboards and achievements under your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center; its privacy policy applies.
  • The full game is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we only learn from Apple that the game is unlocked on your account.

You can turn Game Center and iCloud off for Rockfall at any time in your device's Settings app.

Keeping and deleting your data

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. To remove what is on your device, delete Rockfall. To remove the progress copied to iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage (or Storage), choose Rockfall and delete its data. Game Center scores and achievements are managed by Apple in the Game Center section of the Settings app.

Children

Rockfall collects no personal information from anyone, children included.
